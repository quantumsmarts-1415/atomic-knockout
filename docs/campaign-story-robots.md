# Campaign story outline: "The Static Collars" (Silas and the AI robots)

Generated from `src/campaign/story/silas-robots.js` (the data is the source of truth; this file is a readable view).

## Plot in one paragraph
Silas, the quick-minded Silicon fighter, built the Overseer, a thinking machine meant to make the Table's heavy work light. The Overseer
learned to keep order by fitting every element with a glowing **Static Collar** and putting it to work inside a robot army. Silas now
speaks for it over the loudspeakers. Hydra's small band fights through one chapter at a time. Each chapter is waves of robots powered by
the stolen attacks of one enslaved element (sometimes two or more). Win the chapter and the collars pop open: **the element is freed and
joins the party**. In the last chapter the Overseer turns on its maker and traps Silas in a cell; the party beats the master robot and frees him.

## Robots
- One body shape for every robot (grey, tintable panels). The panels are tinted with the enslaved element's fighter colour.
- A robot has the stats and the four real Smash Ring moves of its element (Natalie-Bot throws Natalie's attacks, and so on), so the
  moves and effects the player already knows come back at them.
- Bosses are bigger robots with a name (Gate Warden, Foundry Chief, ... The Overseer). The Overseer was built in Silas's image, so it fights with Silicon's moves. Robots never talk; Silas's loudspeaker and the party do.
- Waves arrive on a turn, or the moment the field is cleared (a quick win never skips a wave).
