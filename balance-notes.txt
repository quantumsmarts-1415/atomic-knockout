Balance Notes (Lillie & Ben):
- Base hitstun in the engine calculates to at least 340ms (320ms base + percent growth).
- Lillie's `basicDirectional` (Spark Jab) had duration 180ms and cooldown 90ms. This totals 270ms of recovery. Her next attack's active hitbox starts at 25% of the swing (45ms). 270 + 45 = 315ms, which meant the next hit landed 25ms BEFORE the opponent exited hitstun, causing an inescapable infinite true-combo.
- Increased Lillie's cooldown to 280ms. New timing: 180 (duration) + 280 (cooldown) + 45 (startup) = 505ms. The opponent now has a 165ms window to react, block, or dodge before the next hit lands.
- Ben's `basicDirectional` (Precision Strike) had duration 200ms and cooldown 110ms. New timing: 200 (duration) + 110 (cooldown) + 50 (startup) = 360ms. Still slightly too close to a true combo at higher percents.
- Increased Ben's cooldown to 250ms. New timing: 200 + 250 + 50 = 500ms. Breaking the true combo.
