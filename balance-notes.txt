Balance Notes (Lillie & Ben):
- Lillie's `basicDirectional` (Spark Jab) had duration 180ms and cooldown 90ms. This totals 270ms of recovery before the next attack. The base hitstun is 320ms, meaning Lillie could repeatedly attack an opponent before they could recover, causing an infinite combo. Increased cooldown to 160ms (total 340ms) to ensure opponents can recover.
- Ben's `basicDirectional` (Precision Strike) had duration 200ms and cooldown 110ms (total 310ms). Increased cooldown to 140ms (total 340ms) to avoid infinite combos.
