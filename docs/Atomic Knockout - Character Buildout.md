Stats are based on the actual elements. Natural state is the form it most commonly takes at STP (Gas, liquid, solid). Density is radiation defense and affects your speed. It also affects how far you fly when jumping and taking damage. Hardness/Tensile strength is kinetic attack and defense. Boiling point is heat defense. Electrical conductivity is electrical defense and attack. Electronegativity is chemical defense and attack.

Charge move is activated by holding the normal button.
Normal move is activated by the normal button and a direction (either pushed or while moving).
Defense move is activated by holding the special button.
Special move is activated by the special button and a direction (either pushed or while moving).

Combat Rules:
Stats are modifiers to a base stat. 0 means no modifier.
Hardness (Mohs) is kinetic attack. Tensile strength is kinetic defense.
Gases and liquids have 0 kinetic stats. Missing data is 0.
Density is radiation defense. It also sets your speed and how far you fly when jumping and taking damage (denser = slower and heavier).
Boiling point is heat defense. Cold attacks count as heat damage.
Electrical conductivity is electrical attack and defense. Very high and very low conductivity both take less electrical damage. Mid-range conductivity takes the most.
Chemical damage is modified by the difference in electronegativity between the attacker and the defender. Noble gases are immune to chemical damage.
Ghost characters (Rutherfordium through Oganesson) lose HP steadily while playing. They have very weak stats and super strong attacks. Every missing stat is 0.
Controls: Charge = hold normal. Normal = normal + a direction. Defense = hold special. Special = special + a direction.
Move tags: (draft) = suggested move or description, not decided yet. [currently in the game: ...] = what the game does now.

UNITS: Density in g/cm³ (gases at STP, with g/L in brackets). Hardness (kinetic attack) = Mohs scale; Tensile strength (kinetic defense) = ultimate tensile strength in MPa (approximate; depends on purity and processing). Missing data = 0 (no modifier). Boiling point in kelvin (K). Electrical conductivity in MS/m (1 MS/m = 1,000,000 S/m; copper is 59). Electronegativity on the Pauling scale. "(predicted)" or "(estimated)" = no measured value exists.
