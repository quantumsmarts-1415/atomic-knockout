What you're looking at. Nothing was exported or hand-edited — the browser cuts the parts straight out of art-src/hydra-cut.png every time this page loads, using the polygons in tools/hydra-parts.js. Each part records the angle it was drawn at, so the rig can cancel out the render's action pose and hang the limbs neutral before animating them. Elbows and knees are real joints, which is what a one-piece-per-limb cut couldn't do. Hit Game size to see it at the 153px it actually renders at in a match — that 5:1 downscale is what hides the seams.
Known rough edge. Hydra's big background flame doesn't survive slicing. Flame that touches a limb gets cut out with that limb and rides along fine (watch his fists), but the wide aura behind him belonged to arms that have since moved, so it's left stranded. Tick leftover flame to see the problem — it drifts free of the body. The fix isn't more careful cutting: that glow wants to be a code-drawn effect that follows the fists, the same way the game already synthesises its hit-sparks and KO bursts. Off by default because it currently looks worse.
